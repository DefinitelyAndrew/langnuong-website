Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

1. Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive information supplied by Google such as your name, email address, profile image, and account identifier. We also receive information you choose to provide when contacting us or placing an order request.

2. How we use information

We use account information to provide sign-in, save your profile, support shop features, respond to enquiries, and improve the website. We do not sell your personal information or share it with others for their own advertising.

3. Service providers

Account authentication and profile storage are provided through Supabase. Google processes Google sign-in according to Google's privacy policies. The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services.

4. Local browser storage

The shop may save cart items and your remembered email preference in your browser's local storage. This information remains on your device and can be removed by clearing the site's browser data or using the available cart and account controls.

5. Security and retention

We use reasonable measures and trusted service providers to protect account information. We retain information only while it is needed to provide account, shop, or customer-support features, unless a longer period is required by law.

6. Your choices

You may sign out, clear your saved cart, remove your remembered email, or ask us to review or delete your account information. Contact Lang Nuong Vietnam through the contact details on this website to make a request.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or legal requirements change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
