About Bo De Vegetarian

Vietnamese vegetarian cuisine, crafted with mindfulness: clean food, clear mind, conscious life.

The menu includes Vietnamese vegetarian favourites, vegan-marked hotpots with fermented tofu, fruit, mushrooms or Sichuan broth, and mushroom soup. Pineapple fried rice is another highlight.
