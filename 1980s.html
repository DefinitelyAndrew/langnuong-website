Lang Nuong Vietnam 1980's

Lang Nuong 1980's concept derives from the inspiration in providing our patrons Authentic Vietnamese Home-cooked cuisines from all parts of Vietnam.

Our Logo is a combination of a rice clay pot on a charcoal stove. Rice claypot (sometimes translated as "rice casserole") is the traditional way of cooking rice on the Charcoal Stove, giving it a distinctive flavor. This represents that we insist on the most traditional recipes & ingredients in our cuisines so that our diners will be able to enjoy the Authentic Vietnamese Cuisines.

At the same time, we showcase different types of Vietnamese Art pieces as well as allowing our diners to be able to reminisce the History & Culture of Vietnam.

The Arts & History

We not only hope our diners will enjoy the Authentic Vietnamese cuisine, but while dining, they can feel, admire and understand the different types of Arts & some Historic items that we have showcased in our restaurant.

🖼️ Art Pieces

Oil Paintings, Ceramic Paintings, Lacquer Paintings, Silk Embroidered Paintings, Ceramic Pottery/Porcelain and Contemporary Abstract Paintings. Fall in love? They're for sale!

📺 Vintage Items

Vintage TV, rotary telephone, iron, camera, fan, decorations and even rare currencies (notes & coins), stamps, Vietnam War posters — a living museum of 1980s Vietnam.

🍷 Vietnamese Beverages

Proud to be one of the first to serve directly imported Vietnamese Red & White wine from Dalat. Vietnamese Beers (Saigon, 333), our in-house 1980's beer, and spirits.
