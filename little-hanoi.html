About Little Hanoi

In the bustling landscape of commercial chains in Singapore, authentic cultural cuisine can sometimes feel like a rare gem. Yet, nestled beneath the vibrant pulse of Orchard Road lies a Vietnamese culinary haven waiting to be explored.

Little Hanoi, tucked away in the basement food court of Shaw House, is quietly gaining popularity for its dedication to serving up healthy yet indulgent Northern Vietnamese classics.

During the lunch rush, expect a brief wait in line as office workers and food enthusiasts alike flock to savour their favourite dishes. Amidst the hustle, the Vietnamese staff exude warmth and friendliness, adding to the inviting atmosphere. While staples like pho and banh mi are readily available, Little Hanoi surprises with lesser-known delights like bun cha, a tantalising dry noodle dish.

Orchard Road's Hidden Vietnamese Culinary Gem — with prices that won't break the bank, Little Hanoi beckons you to savour the essence of Vietnam without leaving Singapore.

Our Food

Our Specialty

Little Hanoi brings authentic Vietnamese cuisine to Shaw Lido, offering delicious pho and banh mi sandwiches at competitive prices. Highlights include fresh spring rolls, the savory pork belly Banh Mi, and the classic Hanoi Beef Noodle Soup.

🍖 BBQ Pork with Rice Vermicelli

Smoky grilled pork, paired with delicate rice noodles and a hint of sweetness from the red chili sauce — a symphony of flavours.

🥢 Bun Cha

Lesser-known but beloved — tantalising dry noodle dish with grilled pork, fresh herbs, and nuoc cham dipping sauce.

🍜 Hanoi Beef Noodle Soup

Simmered for 12 hours, the rich broth brims with basil-infused notes, while tender slices of beef add depth to each spoonful.

🥖 Viet Style Pork Belly Sandwich

The interplay of pate, xa xiu, and chilli mayonnaise sauce elevates the humble baguette to new heights. A must-try banh mi.

🌿 Fresh Spring Rolls

Packed with succulent prawns and crisp fresh vegetables. A refreshing, light starter bursting with Vietnamese flavour.

☕ Traditional Viet Coffee

Robust aroma and creamy texture — the perfect finale to your meal. Also available iced for the Singapore heat.
